Thirty years behind the tap.

A note on who we are, and why bars, restaurants, and breweries across Alberta trust us with their systems.

Our story

With more than 30 years of experience serving Alberta, Dispensing Systems has built its reputation on practical experience, quality workmanship, and dependable service.

We understand that a draught system isn't simply a collection of parts. Every component needs to work together, from the keg and gas system through the beer line, cooling system, tower, and faucet.

Whether you're building a new bar or keeping an existing system running, DSI provides the experience and technical knowledge to get the job done right.

Trusted by businesses across Alberta

From independent pubs and restaurants to some of Alberta's most recognized hospitality brands, DSI has the experience and infrastructure to support commercial draught systems of all sizes.

We proudly work with established restaurant groups, hospitality companies, and multi-location operators, providing reliable draught system installation, service, maintenance, and equipment support.

Trusted by the beer industry

DSI works closely with breweries, beverage suppliers, and industry partners across Alberta to deliver reliable draught systems from the brewery to the glass.

We work closely with industry leaders including:

Molson Coors Labatt Breweries Big Rock Brewery Asahi Canada Breakthru Beverage

We don't just work in the beer industry, we're part of it.
